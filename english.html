A journey of transformation

Before I Am Used

A journey before discovering our calling.

Before God uses our hands, He wants to work in our hearts.

Before I Am Used is a journey of reflection, transformation, and preparation. It helps us understand that before we become instruments in God’s hands, we must allow Him to work within us.

The book leads readers through a journey of self-examination, surrender, transformation, being filled by God, and ultimately becoming willing to be used by God.

“God’s greatest work is in us, not through us.”

A journey

Explore the chapters

A brief glimpse into the journey presented in Before I Am Used.

CHAPTER 01

Before Anything Else

Before thinking about what God can do through us, we need to understand what He wants to do within us.

CHAPTER 02

Search Me

A journey of honesty before God, allowing Him to reveal what we often hide even from ourselves.

CHAPTER 03

Break Me

Surrender is part of God’s process of shaping a heart within us that is willing to obey.

CHAPTER 04

Transform Me

God is not interested only in changing what we do, but in transforming who we are.

CHAPTER 05

Fill Me

After we are emptied, there is room for what comes from God. Being filled is also part of the preparation.

CHAPTER 06

Use Me

Purpose does not begin when we are used. There is a prior work that must take place within us.
