Before I Am Used

A journey before encountering our calling.

Before God uses our hands, He desires to work in our hearts.

Before I Am Used is a journey of reflection, transformation and preparation to understand that, before we become instruments in God's hands, we need to allow Him to work within us.

The book takes the reader on a journey through confrontation, brokenness, transformation, filling and, finally, a willingness to be used by God.

“God's greatest work is in us, not through us.”
A journey

Chapter Preview

A small preview of the journey proposed in Before I Am Used.

CHAPTER 01

Before Everything

Before thinking about what God can do through us, we need to understand what He desires to do in us.

CHAPTER 02

Search Me

A journey of honesty before God, allowing Him to reveal what we often hide even from ourselves.

CHAPTER 03

Break Me

Brokenness as part of God's process of forming in us a heart willing to obey.

CHAPTER 04

Transform Me

God is not interested only in changing what we do, but in transforming who we are.

CHAPTER 05

Fill Me

After emptiness comes room for what comes from God. Being filled is also part of the preparation.

CHAPTER 06

Use Me

Purpose does not begin when we are used. There is a previous work that needs to take place within us.

Readers' Questions

What is the main message of Before I Am Used?
The central message is to reflect on what God desires to do in us before what He desires to do through us.
Is the book only about ministry calling?
No. The reflection reaches anyone who wants to better understand God's work in their life, character and purpose.
